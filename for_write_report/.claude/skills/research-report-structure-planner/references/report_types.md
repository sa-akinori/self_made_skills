## Scientific & Technical Research Report

**Use for**: Algorithm development, materials science, data science, engineering research, technical analysis

**Core Chapter Pattern**:
1. Executive Summary
2. Background and Objectives
3. Technical/Scientific Analysis (multiple domain-specific chapters)
4. Methodology and Approaches
5. Empirical/Experimental Data (if applicable)
6. Industry Trends and Competitive Landscape
7. Integrated Discussion
8. Conclusions and Recommendations
9. Appendices and References

**Key Features**:
- Heavy emphasis on methodology and data analysis
- Structured approach to technical details
- Integration of multiple scientific domains
- Data-driven conclusions

## Competitive Analysis Report

**Use for**: Competitor assessment, competitive positioning, market intelligence

**Core Chapter Pattern**:
1. Executive Summary
2. Analysis Framework and Methodology
3. Competitive Landscape Overview
4. Individual Competitor Profiles
5. Comparative Analysis (features, pricing, positioning)
6. Competitive Strengths and Weaknesses
7. Market Share and Positioning
8. Implications and Strategic Recommendations
9. Appendices

**Key Features**:
- Detailed competitor profiling
- Comparative matrices
- SWOT analysis
- Strategic implications

## Custom/General Research Report

**Use for**: Unique or multi-disciplinary research that doesn't fit standard categories

**Core Chapter Pattern**:
1. Executive Summary
2. Background and Objectives
3. Research Methodology
4. Findings (organized by research questions/themes)
5. Analysis and Discussion
6. Implications
7. Limitations and Future Research
8. Conclusions and Recommendations
9. Appendices and References

**Key Features**:
- Flexible structure adapted to content
- Clear organization by themes or questions
- Emphasis on analysis and implications
- Acknowledgment of scope and limitations

---

## Adaptation Guidelines

When adapting these patterns to specific user topics:

1. **Merge similar sections**: If topics naturally group together, combine chapters
2. **Add specialized chapters**: For highly technical or unique topics, add domain-specific chapters
3. **Adjust depth**: Match chapter complexity to topic importance and available information
4. **Maintain flow**: Ensure logical progression from background → analysis → conclusions
5. **Consider audience**: Adjust technical depth and business focus based on intended readers
